# Victory Sync client portal

## Sign in and choose your business

Open the invitation link sent by Victory Sync, create your password, and sign in at `portal.victorysync.com`. If you belong to more than one business, use the account selector at the top of the sidebar. On a phone, open the menu to find it. Your account access determines which pages appear.

## Get started

Open **Launchpad** for the setup checklist. Add your business phone, Google profile, Meta account ID, and Google review link in **Settings** with your account manager. The checklist also shows whether a landing page is published and a workflow is active. A client staff member may need the owner or account manager to change settings.

## Daily work

- **Dashboard** shows leads, bookings, calls, reviews, ad spend and goal progress for a selected date range.
- **Leads** shows your account's contacts and pipeline. Open a lead to review activity, add notes, move its stage, or mark a job Won with its value.
- **Calls** shows answered and missed calls, summaries, outcomes and available recordings. Use the date and outcome filters to find a call.
- **Reviews** shows requests and received reviews. Use **Request a review** after the customer has completed a job and supplied a phone number through a form or call.
- **Pages** and **Forms** show campaign assets and results. Page editing is available only when Victory Sync enables it for your account.
- **Reports** contains the live metrics and sent PDF reports.
- **Billing** shows your package and invoices. **Manage billing** opens Stripe's secure customer portal when billing has been connected.

If a page is missing, your role may not include it. Ask the account owner or Victory Sync account manager to review your access. Never share passwords for Google, Meta, or your domain in the CRM; grant partner or manager access through those services instead.

To stop automated texts to a contact, reply `STOP` from that phone or ask Victory Sync to mark the contact do-not-contact. Do not restart automated messages without new documented consent.
